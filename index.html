A few things
before you come.

Still wondering about something?
Give us a call. We’re happy to help.

How much is the entrance fee?

Beach entrance is ₱30 per person. When you enquire about an overnight room, we’ll confirm how entrance applies to your booking. Children below 8 years old have complimentary entrance.

Which rooms have air conditioning?

Rooms 1, 2, 3, 4 and 5 all have air conditioning and a private bathroom. The rest house is fan-cooled and uses shared bathroom facilities.

Can we bring food and cook?

You can prepare meals in our shared outdoor kitchen. Room 3 has its own kitchen. Ask us about the cooking equipment you’ll need before your visit.

Can we stay in a cottage overnight?

Yes. Cottages are ₱800 overnight, with check-in at 1:00 pm and check-out at 12:00 noon. Day-tour cottages are ₱400, from 7:00 am to 5:00 pm. Each cottage accommodates up to 15 guests.

How do we reserve a room or cottage?

Call or message us with your dates, group size and preferred accommodation. We’ll confirm availability, check-in details and booking arrangements directly. The enquiry form below prepares an enquiry you can send through Messenger or SMS; it does not make a confirmed reservation.

Can we bring our own tent?

Yes. Ground rental is ₱75 per tent. Contact us to arrange your camping visit and confirm space for your group.
